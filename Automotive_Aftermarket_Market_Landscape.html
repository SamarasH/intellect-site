Why the prices differ

The observed spread is material even after controlling for fitment and channel

  • The selected 15-brand set spans €6.58 to €14.48 including German VAT and excluding delivery: the highest observed price is 2.2× the lowest.
  • The median is €10.68. Ten of the 15 observations sit between €9.48 and €11.98, indicating a relatively tight mainstream band around the median.
  • RIDEX establishes the visible entry price at €6.58; BLUE PRINT and MEAT & DORIA form the high end of this selected benchmark at €13.88 and €14.48.
  • Implication: brand position can move the ticket materially even when SKU, fitment, retailer, country and tax basis are held constant. This exhibit does not isolate manufacturer margin or product quality.

The complete table answers brand pricing, not seller pricing

  • All 15 rows are observed on pkwteile.de in the same window, so the comparison avoids gaps and cross-retailer differences in promotions, shipping thresholds and storefront pricing.
  • The source page lists 110 compatible products; the declared universe here is the selected 15-brand benchmark shown in full, not every catalogue listing.
  • A seller-by-seller matrix should be added only when the same 15 references can be observed across every declared seller on the same VAT and delivery basis.

Flagged & unverified

  • Prices are point-in-time retailer observations and can change with promotions, stock and delivery thresholds.
  • All prices include 19% German VAT and exclude delivery, as stated on the source page.
  • This is the complete declared 15-brand benchmark on one retailer, not all 110 compatible catalogue items and not a seller-by-seller market comparison.

Key terms

Manufacturer price
The manufacturer's selling price at its own premises — before transport, downstream margins and VAT. Use only when the approved market boundary is manufacturer revenue.
End-user / installed spend
Spending at the final buyer boundary. Hardware, installation, civil works, grid works and tax treatment must match the declared study scope; there is no universal conversion multiple.
Consumer↔manufacturer ratio
The multiple between end-user price and manufacturer price for a channel — used to move a market size between the two bases without distorting it.
Channel
A distinct route a product takes to its buyer (e.g. OEM first-fit vs aftermarket). Price builds up differently per channel, so they are never blended.
Triangulation
Sizing a market by ≥2 independent methods and reconciling them; agreement raises confidence, divergence is itself a finding.
TAM / SAM / SOM
Total / Serviceable / Obtainable market — the whole market, the slice a player can address, and the slice it can realistically win.
Concentration (CR4 / HHI)
How consolidated a market is — CR4 = combined share of the top 4 players; HHI = sum of squared shares.
