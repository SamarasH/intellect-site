Inside the research workflow · 15 AI research & review steps

The full workflow has 22 steps: 15 AI research and review steps, optional competitive pricing, automated data and calculation controls, and scope/publication gates. The number of steps used depends on the study; analytical model recovery currently supports charging studies.

  • Lead plannerAssign questions, sources and deliverables. Failed plan gate stops research.
  • Value-chain agentMap buyers, suppliers, channels, procurement and installed-cost boundaries.
  • Regulation & macro agentIdentify obligations, milestones, criticality and commercial mechanisms.
  • Demand agentBuild the full driver/barrier inventory and demand equations by category.
  • Market-size agentDevelop independent sizing pathways; reconcile scope and assumptions.
  • Industry-structure agentSeparate categories, value pools, supplier revenues and margins.
  • Participants agentMap competitors against comparable segments and denominators.
  • Competitive intelligence · optionalOptional price and seller research when enabled in the run.
  • Independent claim verifierTest source entailment, scope and contradictions for material claims.
  • Targeted claim repairResearch failed claims; re-read sources and reverify changed claims once.
  • Impact reassessmentReassess drivers/barriers after repair. Impact and evidence confidence stay separate.
  • Analytical model recoveryCharging workflow: research, calculate, independent challenge; at most two repair cycles.
  • Expert-evidence panelScout stakeholder strata, verify identities and statements, deduplicate and assess claim positions.
  • Quotation & dissent selectionSelect contextual support and challenge from the verified expert registry.
  • Synthesis agentWrite the consulting story using governed claims, caveats and eligible model outputs.
  • Final editorial reviewOne bounded semantic review of the rendered content against its evidence.

Reported facts, calculated estimates and analyst assumptions remain distinct. Source checking and targeted repair reduce unsupported claims; expert opinion does not establish a market-size number. Public expert statements are labelled separately from interviews conducted for a study.
