How it's built

Define → Research → Challenge → Interpret

A lead agent scopes the market, then specialist agents each web-research one workstream and return source-traced findings. An adversarial verifier tries to refute the decision-critical claims, and only then does a synthesizer write the executive read — from verified claims alone.

1

Approved study scope

Freeze geography, period, measurement basis, exclusions and decision.

2

Lead planner

Assign questions, sources and deliverables. Failed plan gate stops research.

3

Deterministic data pack

Retrieve mapped APIs and datasets; preserve units, periods and provenance.

4

Value-chain agent

Map buyers, suppliers, channels, procurement and installed-cost boundaries.

5

Regulation & macro agent

Identify obligations, milestones, criticality and commercial mechanisms.

6

Demand agent

Build the full driver/barrier inventory and demand equations by category.

7

Market-size agent

Develop independent sizing pathways; reconcile scope and assumptions.

8

Industry-structure agent

Separate categories, value pools, supplier revenues and margins.

9

Participants agent

Map competitors against comparable segments and denominators.

10

Competitive intelligence · optional

Optional price and seller research when enabled in the run.

11

Evidence graph & source reads

Create claim IDs, revisions, dependencies and contradiction clusters; enrich sources.

12

Independent claim verifier

Test source entailment, scope and contradictions for material claims.

13

Targeted claim repair

Research failed claims; re-read sources and reverify changed claims once.

14

Impact reassessment

Reassess drivers/barriers after repair. Impact and evidence confidence stay separate.

15

Analytical model recovery

Charging workflow: research, calculate, independent challenge; at most two repair cycles.

16

Expert-evidence panel

Scout stakeholder strata, verify identities and statements, deduplicate and assess claim positions.

17

Quotation & dissent selection

Select contextual support and challenge from the verified expert registry.

18

Synthesis agent

Write the consulting story using governed claims, caveats and eligible model outputs.

19

Calculations & story architecture

Check arithmetic; assemble answer, proof, visual and implication; route missing evidence.

20

Final editorial review

One bounded semantic review of the rendered content against its evidence.

21

Publication gate

Check completeness, contradictions, evidence freshness, expert coverage and editorial approval.

22

Save candidate / release decision

Keep incomplete work as a draft. Promotion and public export require their applicable gates.

Every figure carries a confidence label:

CONFIRMED — ≥2 independent sources RESEARCHED — one cited source HYPOTHESIS — estimate, shown as a range CONFLICTING — sources disagree
Under the hood

A reusable data stack, not a prompt

The differentiation from a plain LLM is the data layer: 70+ typed clients over public market-intelligence and financial sources (Eurostat, EAFO, ACEA, GLEIF, UN Comtrade, FRED, USGS, USPTO and more), entity resolution, cross-signal triangulation and source-traced records. Research agents combine source retrieval, explicit modelling assumptions and claim-level checks. Unresolved findings remain qualified until independent review is complete.
